Likely bottleneck: Inspection

Educational discrete-event model: stations process one unit at a time. Workers provide a shared labor capacity of 18 units/hour per worker, allocated across the three stations (6 per worker per station). To keep the model useful at different staffing levels, each station's effective capacity is limited by its assigned labor capacity. Downtime reduces processing availability. Parts cannot leave a station when its downstream buffer is full. Simulation runs at 1 simulated minute per real second.
